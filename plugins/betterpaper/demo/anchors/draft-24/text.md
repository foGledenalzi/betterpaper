# The Reed Meter

The reed meter is the best way to measure a river and everybody knows this. Reeds has been used by river people since long ago and they are very good at it. This essay will show why the reed meter is the best and also why rivers are important to history, which is a big subject.

First the reed. You stand a reed in the water and it leans, the more it leans the faster the river is going. Its obvious. Some people say a reed can not be trusted because it bends for other reasons but this is not true, reeds are natural and nature does not lie. A reed that leans is telling the truth about the river and so it is a better measurement than any machine. My uncle had a reed and it worked fine.

Rivers have always been important. Cities grew by rivers and trade went along them and without rivers there would be no history at all. The reed meter is part of this history, so it must be the best. In the old days the people did not have machines, they had reeds, and they did alright. This shows that the old ways are better than the new ways in most things, not only rivers.

Modern meters are expensive and they breaks. A reed costs nothing and grows back every year. Also machines makes people lazy and they stop looking at the water. A person who looks at the water learn more than a person who looks at a dial. I think everyone should have a reed.

In conclusion the reed meter is the best because it is natural, and rivers are important, and the old ways was good. It is clear that the reed meter should be used more and then things would be better for everyone.
