# On the Reading of the Tessel-Glass, second edition (excerpt)

Ismund Karvale. Revised translation. Larkmoor University Press, 1971. The pagination differs from the first edition; the section numbers are the same. Only pages 13, 52 and 71 are given, so a search of this file cannot show that a wording is absent from the edition.

[p. 13] (Section 2) Each mark puts a question to the stack; a reading only answers it.

[p. 52] (Section 9) Where the grit lies level with no mark, the glass puts no question and gives no tally.

[p. 71] (Section 12) A glass is honest when it is read as the question it was cut to ask.
